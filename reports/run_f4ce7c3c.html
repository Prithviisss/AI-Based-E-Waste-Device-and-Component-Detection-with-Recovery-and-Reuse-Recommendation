Limitations

Reuse and recovery potential are determined by a knowledge base because no public dataset provides these labels. Visible condition is limited to a low-confidence manual-inspection flag. RAM and SSD detection is trained on a very small public dataset and is expected to be weaker than PCB part detection. Public images are cleaner than real e-waste photos, so real-world accuracy may be lower. Aluminium is inferred, not detected.

Future work: collect an annotated condition dataset and train XGBoost/Random Forest with SHAP to replace the rules.
