How It Works

01

Device Classification

EfficientNet-B0 identifies the device type from 12 categories trained on the Kaggle E-Waste dataset.

02

Component Detection

Two separate YOLOv8 detectors run in parallel: one for PCB parts, one for RAM & SSD modules.

03

Rule-Based Recovery

A knowledge base with cited figures (UN, WEEE, iFixit) determines the optimal recovery route per component.

04

MILP Optimization

Integer programming selects the best recovery pathways within your budget to maximise value or CO₂ savings.

⚠ Recovery and reuse potential is determined by a knowledge base — not a trained model. Real-world accuracy may be lower than lab metrics. Always consult a certified e-waste recycler.
